# Kare imagegen ön çalışması

İki bağımsız ekran referansı, yerleşik imagegen aracıyla üretildi. CLI kullanılmadı. Araç belirli model sürümü seçimi sunmadığından “imagegen 2.5” sürümü doğrulanamaz. Ekran referansları kare-home.png ve kare-editor.png olarak kaydedildi. Uygulamanın gerçek ekran görüntüleri bunlardan ayrı, canlı editörden alındı.

## Kategori giriş ekranı — kullanılan prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create ONE large, exceptionally clear 1600x1000 desktop web-app category selection screen for a Turkish Canvas animation studio called 'kare'. This is an implementation reference, flat screenshot without monitor/device. Art direction: a real independent animation atelier and motion-design sketchbook, restrained playful personality, crisp typography, deliberate space. Palette: parchment #F6F1E9, dark aubergine ink #392936, muted plum #725367, vermilion #C65339, pale dusty lilac #E8DFE6, butter #E9CB75. No green/teal. Clean expressive rounded grotesk typography with character; comfortable 14px body, 42px title, wide margins ~72px, thin plum dividers, square/8px corners not pills, no shadows. Top left a custom abstract four-corner framing glyph with 'kare' lowercase bold, small 'animasyon atölyesi'. Top right small 'Projeyi aç' and an outlined 'Devam et →' button. Main header left 'Ne canlandırmak istersin?' on two balanced lines, subtitle 'Bir alan seç, fikrine yer aç.' Right of heading a small charming abstract handmade frame-sequence illustration, colored geometric shapes and a sketch line with actual design purpose. Below an elegant open 2-column gallery of FOUR clickable category entries, thumbnails illustrated in actual Canvas-like procedural art styles rather than photos, each thumbnail in a horizontal 16:9 frame ~430x160 and text below with name and small arrow, minimal framing, no nested boxes. Labels exactly: 'Çizim & boya' with watercolor/pastel blooming petal art; 'Hareket & tasarım' with kinetic concentric geometry and a typographic A; 'Doğa & atmosfer' with botanical layered landscape; 'Hikâye & veri' with expressive comic frame and bars. Beneath them one full-width compact butter-tinted row named 'Matematik & eğitim', subtitle 'Ders sahneleri, anlatım ve ses kaydı', with elegant animated graph sketch left and small 'Atölyeyi aç →' right. Bottom subtle text 'Çalışmaların bu tarayıcıda saklanır.' Everything visible at 1000px screen height, about 30% whitespace, readable Turkish text, no wall of controls, no sidebar, no badges, no fake statistics, no gradients, no glossy 3D, no robot or sparkle AI clichés. The app must feel simple, highly usable, ownable and unmistakably about making animation.

## Editör — kullanılan prompt

Girdi: kare-home.png; rolü marka ve görsel dil referansı.

Use case: ui-mockup. The supplied image is a BRAND AND VISUAL LANGUAGE REFERENCE ONLY. Generate a fresh separate 1600x1000 desktop EDITOR SCREEN, not a variation of the category home. Same 'kare' animation atelier identity, four-corner logo, parchment #F6F1E9, aubergine #392936, vermilion #C65339, pale lilac #E8DFE6, butter #E9CB75. Flat sharp readable actual app screenshot, no device. Much calmer editor with progressive disclosure, no left sidebar. Topbar 76px: tiny kare logo left then clickable breadcrumb 'Kategoriler / Çizim & boya', center editable project title 'Çiçeklerin dansı', right quiet 'Geri al' icon and solid aubergine 'Dışa aktar' button. Main content uses large preview area about 72% width plus a right inspector about 280px. Above canvas short scene title 'Çiçeklerin dansı' left, small outlined 'Stil seç ↗' button right. Large 16:9 canvas on a pale dusty lilac workbench, paper background with beautiful evolving watercolor/pastel hand-drawn plum and coral flowers, visual centerpiece with no opaque controls covering art. Canvas is a live drawing not an inserted photo. Immediately below minimal player: play circular coral button, elapsed '0:04 / 0:12', long thin plum scrubber, small 'Kare al'. Then a thin divider and minimal horizontal strip of three real scene thumbnails with names 'Açılış', 'Büyüme', 'Çiçeklenme'; selected second thumbnail has aubergine border, simple '+ Sahne' button to right. Caption 'Sahneler' and contextual move, duplicate, delete actions as quiet text. Right inspector plain, no enclosing card, heading 'Sahne', label and input 'Sahne adı', simple 'Süre' slider showing '12 sn', five square color swatches labelled 'Renkler', color-square background. Below these two CLOSED accordion rows 'İnce ayarlar' and 'Beceri & kod', with subtle chevrons and dividers. At lower right a restrained coral button 'AI ile tasarla' and honest small text 'Bağlantı ayarlanmadı.' No permanently visible prompt textarea, code snippets, seed controls, 20-category list or dense panels. Bottom warm neutral status 'Değişiklikler kaydedildi'. Comfortable text minimum 13px, scene heading26px, alignment and optical spacing disciplined, 24px gutters, only needed controls. Match original reference palette and typographic character. Avoid black/green/teal UI, glossy gradients, fake counters, decorative microbadges, panels inside panels, excessive all-caps text. The editor must feel useful, expressive and deceptively simple.
